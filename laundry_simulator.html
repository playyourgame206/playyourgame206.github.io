📖 How to Play

🏠 Claim a plot — walk to an empty plot and press E (or tap it). You get a free washing machine!

👕 Collect dirty laundry from the carousel with E, tap, or the USE button. Your basket starts at 5 (10 with two machines) — bigger baskets are sold at the shop, and you can never carry more than your machines can wash.

🫧 Fill a machine to the top — the x/y counter above it shows how full it is, and the wash starts by itself when it's full. READY! means the load is clean.

✨ Collect the clean laundry and drop it at the big LAUNDRY DROP chute at the back for points. Rare colours pay more — Platinum pays 16×!

🚪 Cross the road through the EXIT door to visit the shop. Tap machines to fill your cart, or talk to the shopkeeper — sales only happen through them! Watch out for cars…

🧲 Upgrades: the Laundry Magnet (Settings, 25 pts) auto-collects laundry. Bigger machines wash more, faster. After an hour of play everything is 25% off.

🚚 Trucks arrive with 10 customers and a mountain of laundry. Friends on this page at the same time play with you — each on their own plot!

🎮 Controls

💻 Computer
WASD / Arrow keys — walk
SPACE — jump  ·  E — interact
Click things — interact  ·  Mouse wheel — zoom

📱 Tablet / Phone
Touch & drag the LEFT half — walk
Drag the RIGHT half — look around (up/down too!)
Double-tap the right half — jump
Pinch with two fingers — zoom in/out
Tap laundry, machines & doors — interact
JUMP / USE buttons — same as Space / E
