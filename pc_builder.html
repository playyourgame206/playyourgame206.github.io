📖 How to Play

🏝️ Your island — every builder gets their own island. You start with one workbench; buy more (walk to a faint pad, press E) so more PCs can be built at once.

🛒 Collect parts from the PART SHOP — it has tabs for CPUs, GPUs, Motherboards, Power Supplies, SSDs and RAM. The worst parts are FREE — you already own an Intel i3-4170!

🔧 Build — press E at your bench and pick one of each part. But watch out:

🔌 The CPU must fit the board's socket — Socket 1 fits 4th–7th gen, Socket 2 fits 8th–11th gen, Socket 3 fits 12th–14th gen.
⚡ The power supply must cover the CPU + GPU wattage (plus ~75W for the rest).
🎮 A GPU is optional — an office build works without one, but a graphics card makes the PC worth much more!

📦 Sell — grab the finished PC off your bench and carry it to the SHIPPING dock. Better parts = way more money!

👑 The dream PC: an i9-14900KF with an RTX 5090 on a Socket 3 board earns a huge bonus.

👷 Hire workers in the shop's Workers tab! The 🛒 Part Buyer buys a part every cycle, the 🔧 PC Builder starts builds on free tables, and the 🚚 Shipper sells finished PCs. They do one job every 15 seconds — upgrade them (it costs more each time) to work faster, and the Buyer shops for better parts too.

🌀 Visit friends — the 🏝️ Islands button lists everyone online. Teleport to a friend's island to hang out (it always asks "are you sure?" first). Their shop and dock work for you, but only they can use their tables — and your builds and workers keep going while you're away!

🎮 Controls

💻 Computer
WASD / Arrow keys — walk
SPACE — jump  ·  E — interact
Click things — interact  ·  Mouse wheel — zoom

📱 Tablet / Phone
Touch & drag the LEFT half — walk
Drag the RIGHT half — look around (up/down too!)
Double-tap the right half — jump
Pinch with two fingers — zoom in/out
Tap benches, shops & the dock — interact
JUMP / USE buttons — same as Space / E
